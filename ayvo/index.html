What the desk does

Three jobs that take up a lot of an agent’s week. Pick the ones you want help with.

Photo tidy-ups

Brighter, straighter and true-to-colour listing photos. Exposure, white balance, straightening and mild contrast only. We never add furniture, swap skies or hide defects.

Portal-ready listing copy

Descriptions written to the length and style the portals you use expect, built from the facts you give us. You approve every description before it is published.

First-reply drafts

When an enquiry arrives, AI drafts a reply and a person checks it. Your team sends it, so nothing goes out in your name without a human look.

  1. Send us 2 listing photos (or a link to a live listing). Free, no card.
  2. We return the tidied versions side by side with your originals, usually within 2 working days.
  3. If you like them, start a 2-week pilot on your own listings and enquiries. If not, keep the photos anyway.

Questions agents ask

Do you fake or stage anything in the photos?

No. We adjust exposure, white balance, straightness and mild contrast. We do not add or remove objects, replace skies or hide defects.

Does AI send messages to my clients?

No. AI drafts and a person checks. Your team decides what is sent. The after-hours receptionist, when it is live, will be set up only with your approval and will hand conversations back to your team.

Which countries do you work with?

We work remotely with agents in the UK, the UAE, Canada, Kenya and Australia from our office at iHIT, Kilimani, Nairobi.

Who is behind AYVO?

AYVO is a brand of Trublshut, an African-owned technology company founded by Christopher Ojo, Founder & Principal. Trublshut also builds Nota54 for freight forwarders.
